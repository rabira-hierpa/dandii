# Dandii Contributor License Agreement

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **Dandii**, a public transport
route management system for Addis Ababa, maintained by **Rabira Hierpa**
("the Maintainer").

This document contains two agreements:

- [**Individual CLA**](#individual-contributor-license-agreement) — sign this if
  you are contributing as yourself.
- [**Entity CLA**](#entity-contributor-license-agreement) — sign this if you are
  contributing on behalf of a company, agency, university, or other organization.

You only need to sign **once**. It covers all of your past and future
contributions to Dandii. See [How to sign](#how-to-sign) at the bottom.

## Why Dandii needs this

Dandii is released to the public under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](./LICENSE) — free to study, modify, and
share for noncommercial purposes, with credit.

The Maintainer also intends to offer Dandii to transit agencies, operators, and
other organizations under separate **commercial license terms**. That dual
model only works if the Maintainer holds the necessary rights to *every* line of
the codebase. Without a signed CLA, a contribution stays under the
noncommercial license, and the Maintainer cannot include it in a commercial
license — which would eventually force contributed code to be torn out and
rewritten.

**You keep the copyright in your work.** This agreement grants the Maintainer a
license to use it, including under other license terms. It is not an assignment
and it does not take your code away from you.

## How to sign

Signing is a one-time step and takes about a minute.

1. **Open a pull request** that adds a single line to
   [`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](./CONTRIBUTORS.md), in this format:

   ```
   - Full Name <email@example.com> — GitHub @username — ICLA — YYYY-MM-DD
   ```

   Use `ECLA` instead of `ICLA` if you are signing the Entity agreement, and add
   the entity name:

   ```
   - Full Name <email@example.com> — GitHub @username — ECLA, Acme Transit Ltd — YYYY-MM-DD
   ```

2. In that pull request's description, include the sentence:

   > I have read the Dandii Contributor License Agreement in CLA.md and I agree
   > to it for all of my past and future contributions to Dandii.

3. Tick the CLA box in the pull request template on **every** contribution PR
   after that.

Adding your name to `CONTRIBUTORS.md` and making that statement constitutes your
electronic signature and has the same effect as a handwritten signature.

If your legal or procurement team requires a countersigned document instead,
email <rabira.hierpa@gmail.com> and a PDF will be arranged.

### Questions

If anything here is unclear, or the terms are a problem for your employer, open
an issue or email <rabira.hierpa@gmail.com> **before** writing code. It is much
easier to resolve a licensing question up front than after a large pull request
is already open.
